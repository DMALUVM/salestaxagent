## 3. Register for Pennsylvania Sales Tax — CONTESTED FBA Nexus

PA nexus from FBA inventory is CONTESTED. The Online Merchants Guild v. Hassell ruling found FBA inventory alone insufficient for nexus. However, economic nexus ($100k direct sales) is clear. Most practitioners recommend registration as the conservative approach.

### Step 1: ⚠ Evaluate with CPA before registering

PA FBA nexus is the most actively contested position nationwide. The 2022 Commonwealth Court ruling found that FBA inventory alone, without purposeful direction by the seller, was insufficient to establish nexus. However: (1) the ruling is fact-specific, (2) PA DOR may distinguish your case, (3) economic nexus ($100k) is separate and clear. Consult a CPA before deciding.

**Common pitfalls:**
  - ⚠ Registering may create obligations retroactively if DOR interprets your facts differently.
  - ⚠ NOT registering carries risk if DOR asserts nexus despite the OMG ruling.

### Step 2: If registering: apply via PA e-TIDES

Register as a remote seller through PA's e-TIDES online system. Apply for a Sales Tax License.

🔗 [PA e-TIDES](https://www.etides.state.pa.us/)
