## 3. Register for Vermont Sales Tax

If you have physical nexus (FBA inventory) or economic nexus ($100,000 OR 200 transactions (includes marketplace sales)) in Vermont, registration is required.

### Step 1: Register with Vermont DOR

Apply as a remote seller. Threshold: $100,000 OR 200 transactions (includes marketplace sales). FBA inventory likely creates nexus (majority rule; no specific carve-out found).

🔗 [VT Dept of Taxes](https://tax.vermont.gov/)

**Documents needed:**
  - Federal EIN
  - LLC formation certificate
  - Responsible party SSN
**✓ Done when:** Receive a Vermont sales tax permit/license number.

**Deadline:** 25th of following month (verify with DOR)

## 4. Amazon Marketplace — Vermont

Amazon collects and remits VT sales tax on marketplace orders. This does not eliminate registration or filing obligations.

### Step 1: Amazon handles sales tax on Amazon orders

You do not owe additional sales tax on Amazon-facilitated orders.


### Step 2: Register and file if nexus exists

If you have physical or economic nexus, registration may be required. Returns show Amazon-collected amounts as marketplace deductions.


### Step 3: Collect on Shopify/direct

You are responsible for collecting and remitting on direct (Shopify) orders shipped to VT.
