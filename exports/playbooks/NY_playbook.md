## 3. Register for New York Sales Tax

If you have physical nexus (FBA inventory) or economic nexus ($500,000 AND 100 transactions (includes marketplace sales)) in New York, registration is required.

### Step 1: Register with New York DOR

Apply as a remote seller. Threshold: $500,000 AND 100 transactions (includes marketplace sales). State has marketplace-facilitator FBA carve-out — FBA inventory alone may not create nexus.

🔗 [NYS DTF](https://www.tax.ny.gov/)

**Documents needed:**
  - Federal EIN
  - LLC formation certificate
  - Responsible party SSN
**✓ Done when:** Receive a New York sales tax permit/license number.

### Step 2: Note filing frequency and due dates

Typical: quarterly filing. Returns typically due the 20th of the month following the period. Verify assigned frequency after registration.

**Deadline:** 20th of following month (verify with DOR)
**Frequency:** Quarterly

### Step 3: Configure Shopify and update dashboard

Enable NY tax collection in Shopify for direct orders. Amazon handles marketplace orders. Mark NY as registered in the dashboard.


**Official links:**
- [registration_url](https://www.tax.ny.gov/)

## 4. Amazon Marketplace — New York

Amazon collects and remits NY sales tax on marketplace orders. This does not eliminate registration or filing obligations.

### Step 1: Amazon handles sales tax on Amazon orders

You do not owe additional sales tax on Amazon-facilitated orders.


### Step 2: Register and file if nexus exists

If you have physical or economic nexus, registration may be required. Returns show Amazon-collected amounts as marketplace deductions.


### Step 3: Collect on Shopify/direct

You are responsible for collecting and remitting on direct (Shopify) orders shipped to NY.
