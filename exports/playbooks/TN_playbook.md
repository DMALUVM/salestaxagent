## 4. Tennessee Franchise & Excise Tax

TN imposes franchise tax (on net worth/property) and excise tax (6.5% on net earnings) on entities doing business in TN. Minimum franchise tax is $100.

### Step 1: Review entity-level obligations with CPA

Tennessee franchise & excise tax may apply to entities doing business in TN. The excise tax is on net earnings; franchise tax is on net worth or book value of real/tangible property in TN.


## 5. Amazon Marketplace — Tennessee

Amazon collects and remits TN sales tax on marketplace orders. This does not eliminate registration or filing obligations.

### Step 1: Amazon handles sales tax on Amazon orders

You do not owe additional sales tax on Amazon-facilitated orders.


### Step 2: Register and file if nexus exists

If you have physical or economic nexus, registration may be required. Returns show Amazon-collected amounts as marketplace deductions.


### Step 3: Collect on Shopify/direct

You are responsible for collecting and remitting on direct (Shopify) orders shipped to TN.
