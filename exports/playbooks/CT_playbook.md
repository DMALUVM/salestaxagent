## 3. Register for Connecticut Sales Tax

If you have physical nexus (FBA inventory) or economic nexus ($100,000 AND 200 transactions (includes marketplace sales)) in Connecticut, registration is required.

## 4. Amazon Marketplace — Connecticut

Amazon collects and remits CT sales tax on marketplace orders. This does not eliminate registration or filing obligations.

### Step 1: Amazon handles sales tax on Amazon orders

You do not owe additional sales tax on Amazon-facilitated orders.


### Step 2: Register and file if nexus exists

If you have physical or economic nexus, registration may be required. Returns show Amazon-collected amounts as marketplace deductions.


### Step 3: Collect on Shopify/direct

You are responsible for collecting and remitting on direct (Shopify) orders shipped to CT.
