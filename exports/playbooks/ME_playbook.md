## 3. Register for Maine Sales Tax

If you have physical nexus (FBA inventory) or economic nexus ($100,000 (direct sales only; marketplace excluded)) in Maine, registration is required.

### Step 1: Register with Maine DOR

Apply as a remote seller. Threshold: $100,000 (direct sales only; marketplace excluded). FBA inventory likely creates nexus (majority rule; no specific carve-out found).

🔗 [ME Revenue Services](https://www.maine.gov/revenue/)

**Documents needed:**
  - Federal EIN
  - LLC formation certificate
  - Responsible party SSN
**✓ Done when:** Receive a Maine sales tax permit/license number.

### Step 2: Note filing frequency and due dates

Typical: monthly filing. Returns typically due the 15th of the month following the period. Verify assigned frequency after registration.

**Deadline:** 15th of following month (verify with DOR)

### Step 3: Configure Shopify and update dashboard

Enable ME tax collection in Shopify for direct orders. Amazon handles marketplace orders. Mark ME as registered in the dashboard.

## 4. Amazon Marketplace — Maine

Amazon collects and remits ME sales tax on marketplace orders. This does not eliminate registration or filing obligations.

### Step 1: Amazon handles sales tax on Amazon orders

You do not owe additional sales tax on Amazon-facilitated orders.


### Step 2: Register and file if nexus exists

If you have physical or economic nexus, registration may be required. Returns show Amazon-collected amounts as marketplace deductions.


### Step 3: Collect on Shopify/direct

You are responsible for collecting and remitting on direct (Shopify) orders shipped to ME.
