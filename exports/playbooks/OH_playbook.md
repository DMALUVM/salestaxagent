## 4. Ohio Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)

Ohio CAT is a gross receipts tax at 0.26% on receipts over $1M. Minimum $150 annual tax for businesses with $150k-$1M in OH gross receipts. FBA inventory may create CAT nexus.

### Step 1: Determine if CAT applies

If your Ohio gross receipts exceed $150,000, the CAT minimum tax ($150) applies. If over $1M, the 0.26% rate applies. CAT has its own nexus provisions (bright-line test: $500k receipts or $50k payroll/property in OH).

**Common pitfalls:**
  - ⚠ CAT is separate from sales tax.
  - ⚠ Interaction between FBA inventory and CAT nexus is not fully settled — confirm with CPA.

### Step 2: Register for CAT if applicable

Register via Ohio Business Gateway. Annual minimum tax ($150) due May 10. Quarterly estimated payments if annual CAT exceeds $1,500.

🔗 [Ohio Business Gateway](https://gateway.ohio.gov/)

**Deadline:** Annual minimum due May 10; quarterly estimates if > $1,500
