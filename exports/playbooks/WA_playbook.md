## 3. Register for Washington Sales Tax

FBA inventory or $100k+ sales (includes marketplace) creates nexus. Register for a sales tax certificate with WA DOR.

### Step 1: Apply via WA DOR Business Licensing Service

Register online through the WA DOR Business Licensing portal. You will need your Federal EIN, LLC details, and estimated WA sales.

🔗 [WA DOR Business Licensing](https://dor.wa.gov/open-business/apply-business-license)

**Documents needed:**
  - Federal EIN
  - LLC formation certificate
  - Responsible party SSN
**✓ Done when:** Receive a WA UBI number and sales tax certificate.

### Step 2: Note filing frequency

WA assigns monthly, quarterly, or annual filing. Returns due the 25th of the month following the period. Combined return covers both sales tax AND B&O tax on a single form.

**Deadline:** 25th of following month
**✓ Done when:** Filing frequency visible in My DOR account.

### Step 3: Configure Shopify and update dashboard

Enable WA tax in Shopify. Amazon handles marketplace orders. Update Registrations page.

## 4. Washington B&O (Business & Occupation) Tax

IMPORTANT: WA B&O tax is a gross receipts tax SEPARATE from sales tax. It is NOT covered by Amazon marketplace facilitator collection. FBA inventory creates B&O nexus. Retailing B&O rate is 0.471% on gross receipts.

**⚠ CRITICAL** — Do not delay action on this item.

### Step 1: Understand B&O is separate from sales tax

B&O tax applies to your gross receipts from business in WA. Amazon handles sales tax collection, but B&O is your own obligation. The retailing B&O rate is 0.471%. Multiple classifications may apply.

**Common pitfalls:**
  - ⚠ Most commonly overlooked WA obligation for FBA sellers.
  - ⚠ B&O applies even if Amazon collects sales tax.

### Step 2: File B&O on combined return

B&O and sales tax are filed on the same combined excise tax return in My DOR. Due the 25th of the month following the period.

🔗 [WA DOR File & Pay](https://dor.wa.gov/file-pay-taxes)


### Step 3: Small business credit

The small business B&O credit offsets the first ~$125k/month in taxable income (effectively ~$250/month credit for retailing B&O). Most small sellers owe very little after the credit, but filing is still required.

**✓ Done when:** B&O line shows on your combined return with credit applied.

**Official links:**
- [help_url](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/business-occupation-tax)
